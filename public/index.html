CONNECTION STATUS / CHECKED 14 OCT 2026

One login is the idea.
Official access comes first.

The official course login redirects to NTU’s SAML/ADFS SSO service. A campus login doesn’t by itself give this app permission to retrieve a personal timetable.

Live integration is stubbed.
No public third-party OAuth enrollment or personal timetable API was verified in the official sources reviewed. This demo has no authorization to connect. That is not proof that an approved partnership is impossible.

What works here today

Fictional demo login, local CSV/TSV import, timetable views, clash detection, and CSV export. No password fields, cookies, tokens, or NTU session access.

Official places to start

  • myNTU portal ↗ Campus services and personal access
  • NTU course service ↗ Course browsing and your official course login
  • NTU SSO integration background ↗ Official 2013 SAML/ADFS architecture article

Opening an official site is independent of this demo. We do not receive its login or timetable. See the project documentation for the integration requirements.
