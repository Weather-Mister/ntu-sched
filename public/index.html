Required: course, day, start, end. Room and code are optional. Use Monday–Sunday (or 星期一–星期日) and 24-hour times, e.g. 09:10. This template is our format; arbitrary NTU page text and PDFs are not supported.

YOUR NTU CONNECTION

Your timetable, connected.

Campus Week reads your current weekly timetable from NTU ePortfolio using your own account. Your saved connection and timetable are encrypted and belong to your Campus Week account.

Timetables check for changes every five days. Use the timetable refresh button whenever you want a fresh check. If NTU is unavailable, your last successful timetable stays available.

Timetables show a recurring week. Confirm semester dates, holidays and one-off cancellations with NTU. Courses without fixed weekly times cannot be placed on the timetable.

COOL homework connects through your saved NTU login and checks its calendar every five minutes while this site is open. Announcements and the inbox are the next connections. Campus Week is an independent student tool, not an official NTU service.
